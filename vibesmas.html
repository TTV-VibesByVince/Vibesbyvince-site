Format

Five Zero Build Battle Royale games. Points from every game add up to one final score, and a live leaderboard is shown on stream the whole time.

  • Open to all Twitch streamers. Duos only, and both players must be 21 or older.
  • At least one player on each duo streams the tournament live on Twitch.
  • Be checked in and ready by 9:45 AM MST (8:45 PST · 10:45 CST · 11:45 EST).
  • The custom matchmaking key for each game is posted in Discord before every round.
  • No substitutes once the tournament starts.

Rules

  • No teaming.
  • No hacks or system mods.
  • Twitch VODs must stay available after your stream.
  • Both players must be 21 or older.
  • Each duo needs at least one player on Twitch (who streams) and one on Discord (who checks in and posts screenshots). It can be the same person.
  • Rules can change or be added at any time, so keep an eye on Discord. There are no weapon bans right now, but a weapon could be banned in a later game if needed.

After the final game

Winners are announced as soon as possible. Then there is a 24-hour window to raise concerns about misconduct such as cheating, mods or hacks. Concerns need evidence, which is why VODs must stay up.

After 24 hours the scores are final and Vince reaches out to the winners to issue payment.
