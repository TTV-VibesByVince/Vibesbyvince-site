VibesClipper

VibesClipper is a personal, self-hosted tool made by VibesByVince (twitch.tv/vibesbyvince) for his own channel. It runs on his own computer.

What it does

  • Downloads the recordings of his own Twitch streams.
  • Finds the funny and memorable moments.
  • Turns them into vertical short clips with captions.
  • After he reviews and approves each clip, uploads it to his own YouTube channel as a YouTube Short.

Who can use it

Only VibesByVince. VibesClipper is not a public service: nobody else can sign up or connect their account.
