Privacy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Short version: this site doesn't use cookies, doesn't have accounts or forms, and doesn't track you. Here's everything that happens when you visit.

What this site collects

Nothing directly. There are no sign-ups, comments, forms or cookies, and nothing is saved in your browser's storage. If you email me through the "business inquiries" button, I only get what you choose to send, and I use it only to reply.

Visitor stats

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which provides basic, cookie-free visitor counts (Cloudflare Web Analytics), such as page views and which country visits come from. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't follow you to other sites and doesn't identify you personally. Like any web host, Cloudflare also keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses) to keep the site running and protected from attacks. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Other services the page loads from

To show live info, your browser fetches a few things from other services. Like any website, they see your IP address and browser type when that happens:

  • DecAPI (decapi.me): checks whether I'm live on Twitch right now.
  • YouTube (ytimg.com): the thumbnail for my latest upload.
  • TikTok (TikTok's image servers): the cover image for my latest TikTok, when one is featured.

Fonts are hosted on this site, so loading the page doesn't contact Google Fonts.

Links to other sites

Links to Twitch, TikTok, YouTube, Discord, Instagram, Bluesky and GitHub take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

The "vibes setup" link is an affiliate link to Govee. If you buy through it I may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Govee and its affiliate partner may set their own cookies once you're on their site; this site doesn't.

Kids

This site doesn't knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
