Privacy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Short version: this site doesn't use cookies, doesn't have accounts or sign-ups, and doesn't track you. Here's everything that happens when you visit.

What this site collects

Nothing that's kept. There are no sign-ups, comments or cookies, and nothing is saved in your browser's storage. If you email me through the "business inquiries" button, I only get what you choose to send, and I use it only to reply.

Stream schedule

The stream schedule is public and needs no account. If you use "Add to my calendar", your calendar app (Google, Apple, Outlook…) checks this site's schedule feed every few hours; like any website, the site sees that request. The schedule's editing page is only for me and is protected by Cloudflare Access sign-in.

Fortnite stats lookup

On the Fortnite Hub, if you look up a player, the Epic username and platform you type are sent from this site's server to fortnite-api.com to fetch that player's public stats. The result is cached for a few minutes so repeat lookups are fast, but usernames aren't stored, logged or shared with anyone else by this site. Only stats the player has made public in Fortnite can be shown.

The Item Shop, map and item pictures also come from fortnite-api.com, but they're fetched by this site's server, so your browser never contacts fortnite-api.com directly.

Visitor stats

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which provides basic, cookie-free visitor counts (Cloudflare Web Analytics), such as page views and which country visits come from. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't follow you to other sites and doesn't identify you personally. Like any web host, Cloudflare also keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses) to keep the site running and protected from attacks. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Other services the page loads from

To show live info, your browser fetches a few things from other services. Like any website, they see your IP address and browser type when that happens:

  • DecAPI (decapi.me): checks whether I'm live on Twitch right now.
  • YouTube (ytimg.com): the thumbnail for my latest upload.
  • TikTok (TikTok's image servers): the cover image for my latest TikTok, when one is featured.

Fonts are hosted on this site, so loading the page doesn't contact Google Fonts.

Links to other sites

Links to Twitch, TikTok, YouTube, Discord, Instagram, Bluesky and GitHub take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

The Govee ("vibes setup") and Razer links are affiliate links. If you buy through them I may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Those stores and their affiliate partners may set their own cookies once you're on their sites; this site doesn't.

Kids

This site doesn't knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
